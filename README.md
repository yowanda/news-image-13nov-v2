# World News — Friday 13 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026.

The image is a 2x2 collage of the day's four biggest stories, under the
header "FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER 2026":

- **NVIDIA CEO RESIGNS — shares -16%**: a semiconductor chip, the day's
  biggest market shock.
- **UKRAINE TALKS: Europe gets a seat**: a diplomatic handshake, Europe
  joining the table.
- **MICROSOFT 365 DOWN worldwide**: a dark office of empty monitors, the
  global outage.
- **HORMUZ: tankers hit**: an oil tanker at dusk, the shipping flashpoint.

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and verifies it byte-for-byte with
SHA-256 before reporting success.
